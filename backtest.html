How good is the model?

Every week since 2016/17, the model was trained only on matches played before that week, then asked to predict that week's games. Its forecasts are compared with the closing betting market: the odds just before kickoff, with the bookmaker's margin removed, and widely considered the most accurate public forecast there is.
