How this works

Every remaining game gets a random result from the model: its expected margin (team ratings, home field and the expected quarterbacks) plus the usual game-to-game spread. Each simulated season also shifts every team's rating by a random amount (typically about {{ rating_sd }} points), because ratings are estimates; without that, the odds come out too sure of themselves.

Division winners and seeds follow the main NFL tiebreakers: head-to-head, division or conference record, then point differential. The full rules have more steps, but these settle almost every real tie. Then the playoffs are played out the same way.

Tested on 2018–2025 after weeks 4, 8 and 12: teams given 20–40% made the playoffs 35% of the time, 40–60% 52%, and 60–80% 67%. A dash means it didn't happen in any simulation; that's not the same as being eliminated.
