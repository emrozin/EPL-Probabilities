Quarterback adjustments

Each team's expected starter for its next game, and how many points per game the model adds or takes away for him. Quarterbacks with few career starts are rated lower (by up to {{ inexperience }} points for a first start), fading out by {{ inexperience_starts }} starts.

This isn't a ranking of the best quarterbacks. A long-time starter's quality is mostly already in his team's offense rating, so established starters sit near zero. What the adjustment captures is how much better or worse his teams have scored with him than with their other quarterbacks, which is what matters when a backup, a rookie or a new signing starts.
