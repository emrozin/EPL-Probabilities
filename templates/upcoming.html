How to read this

The big numbers are the model's chance of a home win, a draw and an away win. The thick bar shows the same split; the thin bar under it is the betting market's view, from bookmakers' odds with their margin removed. The table puts the two side by side, with the chance of more than 2.5 goals.

A tag like Model 7 pts higher on Arsenal marks a match where the model gives a team a chance of winning at least 5 points higher than the market does. Each team is shown in its own colors; when two are too alike, the away team wears its second color.
